Please check your statement of entry to verify accuracy of registration details, thus name, date of birth, subjects registered for and syllabus option such as core and extended.
Notify ECoL immediately if you find errors in your statement. If there are no errors in your entry, keep all your papers safely because you will be expected to bring them to the examination centre, every time you sit for examination.
You will be expected to produce identification for every sitting. If you do not have any form of identification (National ID, Passport or Driving License), go to the office of Home Affairs to apply for one before the start of the examination series.
Candidates are responsible for making sure that they know the start and finish time for each examination.
Candidates are expected to bring into the examination room only those materials permitted by subject regulations.
Candidates must not have any unauthorised materials in their possession.
Candidates must follow instructions of the invigilator.
Candidates must not be involved in any unfair or dishonest practice before, during or after the examination.
Kindly note that results should be collected as soon as they are published. Queries about results should be lodged to ECoL within a month after publication of results.
